About Me

I'm a first-year Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Strathclyde. This portfolio documents the CAD and design work I build outside of coursework — personal projects, practice assemblies, and design challenges.

My approach is to design things that are manufacturable and grounded in real constraints — not just pretty renders. I'm interested in mechanism design, structural analysis, and how parts actually go together.

I built this portfolio to track my progress and share work I'm proud of. Everything here was made from scratch.
